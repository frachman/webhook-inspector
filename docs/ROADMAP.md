# Public Roadmap

This is a high-level product roadmap, not a commitment to dates or commercial
features.

## Near term

- Privacy-preserving usage analytics: measure homepage visits, endpoint
  creation, webhook receipt, and endpoint views with anonymous daily aggregates;
  pair product events with privacy-friendly website traffic analytics (such as
  Plausible or Umami) so adoption and real usage can be reported separately.
- External alerts for health and backup failures (implementation in `deploy/`)
- Scheduled restore drills
- Request replay and export
- Webhook signature verification

## Later

- Custom response status, body, and delay controls
- Live request updates
- Persistent endpoints and multi-device history
- Team workspaces and collaboration

Account features will be evaluated when persistent history, collaboration, or
paid plans become product requirements. Pricing, revenue targets, and other
financial planning remain private.
